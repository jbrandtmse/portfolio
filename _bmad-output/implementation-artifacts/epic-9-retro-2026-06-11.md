1. **Credibility/outcome tests asserted on the INTERMEDIATE, not the user-observable artifact — TWICE (→ Rule 18).** 9.1 rendered visitor-visible stale build-stats ("nine epics"→10, "Fourteen rules"→17) that passed because the credibility test only grepped `[OPEN:`/ADR, not the rendered counts; 9.3's EPK credibility tests asserted on the intermediate `EpkData`, not the rendered PDF (a render-step flag-strip would have passed). QA caught both by re-asserting on the rendered artifact (served HTML / decoded PDF). → **Rule 18** (assert credibility + outcome on the RENDERED, user-observable artifact — served HTML, the downloaded file's bytes — never an intermediate or a narrow grep; derive stale-able facts so they can't drift; class-guard the category).
2. **A cross-artifact quote drift slipped to code-review (9.1).** The stage-1 narration quoted a positioning line ("Seasoned, building at the frontier") that the LINKED brief reader doesn't contain (it says "Seasoned, Not Stuck" — the line was PRD-refined later). Caught by code-review reading the actual linked artifact. Folded into Rule 18's "audit against the source the claim links to."
