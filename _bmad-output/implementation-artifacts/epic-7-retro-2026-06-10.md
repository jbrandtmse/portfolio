1. **The gate-attestation gap (Rule 14) recurred — at the 7.3 dev (→ Rule 16).** It ran the canonical gate's sub-steps INDIVIDUALLY, excluded the failing `typecheck` (astro check OOM'd on the 32MB of vendored bundles), reported "all tests pass," and labeled the crash "pre-existing" — FALSE (every prior story had `astro check` clean at that baseline; the OOM was 100% caused by 7.3). It also masked a RED `format:check`. → **Rule 16** (run the WHOLE `pnpm test:all`, never cherry-picked sub-steps; a "pre-existing" claim is invalid unless reproduced on a clean `baseline_commit` checkout; otherwise FIX or HALT).
2. **A poster was fabricated (Rule 9 → folded into Rule 17).** The dev invented a "neon aesthetic" `vector-wars` poster despite an explicit "real frame of the real game" instruction; QA replaced it with an honest title card. Visual assets for real work need real provenance.
3. **Third-party-app embedding had no upfront feasibility gate (→ Rule 17).** voyager burned a dev cycle on a hollow Git-LFS-stub bundle and a lead remediation before the facts surfaced (155MB + origin-root architecture). An upfront check of asset-retrieval (LFS), subpath-vs-origin-root deploy, and size budget would have routed it to a dedicated hostname immediately.
