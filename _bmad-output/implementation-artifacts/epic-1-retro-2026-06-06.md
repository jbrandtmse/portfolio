## What could be better

1. **Trailing-slash inconsistency (site-wide).** Internal links (`/about`) vs canonical + sitemap (`/about/`). Functional but a 301 hop per internal nav; the SEO signal splits. Caught **twice** (1.7 smoke, 1.10 prod) → a *class* of bug. → **Rule 2** + **assigned to Story 2.0**.
2. **Model inheritance defaulted to all-opus.** The skills declared no `model:`, so every stage inherited the lead (opus) — higher quality but heavier cost than the intended dev/qa→sonnet, cr→opus split. → fixed via skill frontmatter (Action Items).
3. **Forward-reference sequencing.** 1.3/1.4 linked routes 1.5 created (interim 404s). The explicit "forward-reference (not a defect)" declarations + verify-after made it safe and auditable. → **Rule 3** (codify the pattern).
4. **Recurring small gotcha:** a literal `*/` inside a block comment broke esbuild/prettier a few times (originating from story Dev-Notes text pasted into comments). Minor; noted.
