Design thesis

The medium is the message.

Why this portfolio is built as a public BMAD project, why the Advocate Agent only ever routes to crawlable mirror pages, and how proof-as-process replaces the usual "trust me."

Most portfolios assert. They list roles, name technologies, and ask you to take the rest on faith. That posture fails the one reader who matters here: a skeptical peer who has seen a hundred sites claim depth and has learned to discount the claim. So this site does not argue that the work is good. It shows the work being made, in the open, and lets the record carry the weight. The thesis is narrow and the whole build commits to it: demonstrate the craft by exposing the disciplined process that produced it, rather than describing the outcome after the fact.

That choice has a structural consequence. If the process is the proof, the process has to be legible, and it has to be real. This site is itself a BMAD project, planned and shipped with the same artifacts you are reading now. The brief, the brainstorm, the research, the requirements, the retrospectives as they accrue, are not marketing about the work. They are the work, rendered as a curated read. We call that surface the Glass Box, and its rule is simple: polished glass, not raw guts.

Why the Advocate routes, never invents

The Advocate Agent is the front door, but it is deliberately not the source of truth. Every substantive claim it makes is grounded in a build-time index of the project's own documents, and every claim names and links the real page behind it. Ask it what was hardest about the loandemo build and it answers in plain language, then hands you the retrospective. When the record is silent, it says so and stops; it does not reach for the model to fill the gap.

This is a design decision before it is a safety one. An agent that can route is an agent a reader can verify, and verifiability is the entire pitch. The skeptic does not have to believe the assistant. They can click through and read the artifact for themselves, in the initial HTML, with the script disabled if they like. Routing to crawlable mirror pages means the proof survives every degraded path: no JavaScript, reduced motion, or a search crawler that never runs the agent at all.

The skeptic gets sold, and gets the receipts.

What the curation removes

"Polished, not raw" is a content decision, and it has a default-deny shape. A publish allowlist decides what becomes a public read; everything else stays private by construction. The never-render set is explicit and includes the working scaffolding that would only add noise:

  • Decision logs and internal addenda — the backstage record, not the exhibit.
  • Review and reconciliation notes — useful while building, distracting while reading.
  • Anything not on the allowlist, because the default is to withhold, not to ship.

The result is a read that respects the visitor's attention. Process transparency stays honest about how the work was actually made, while the narrative around it is curated like a highlight reel rather than a confessional. Those two kinds of openness are kept distinct on purpose, and holding both at once is the editorial discipline this surface exists to demonstrate.
