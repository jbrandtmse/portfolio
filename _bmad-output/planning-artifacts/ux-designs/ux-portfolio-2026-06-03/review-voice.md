# Voice & Consistency Review — portfolio

Reviewer: Voice & Content-Consistency. Scope: canonical strings, terminology, microcopy voice, and cross-spine consistency across `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md` (the contract). Critique only — no edits made. Read: `EXPERIENCE.md` (esp. Voice and Tone), `DESIGN.md` (Brand & Style), `.decision-log.md` (canonical-strings decisions). Spines grepped exhaustively for canonical and superseded strings.

## Overall verdict

The contract is **strong**. The canonical strings — *Joshua R. Brandt, MSE*; *"Seasoned, building at the frontier"*; the *Guide* (entry *"Ask my Guide"*); *the BMAD Method* (bare "BMAD" only in coined compounds) — are used consistently and correctly across both spines, with **zero superseded-string leaks**: every occurrence of "Advocate" / "Josh Brandt" / "Seasoned, Not Stuck" / bare "BMAD" is confined to the explicit provenance note or to prose that documents the rename, never presented as canonical. No exclamation marks appear in any authored copy, and no hype/superlative/vendor-speak slips through; the Guide's voice is internally consistent and matches "confident, never hype; guides rather than advocates." The only blemishes are a small set of cross-spine microcopy divergences in the Guide's grounded-assurance and thinking-state lines (neither spine cross-references the other), which leave a builder mildly unsure whether two strings are one element or two — none of which is contract-breaking.

## Findings

### Medium

**1. Guide "grounded" assurance microcopy diverges between the two spines, uncited.**
`DESIGN.md` specifies the Guide **panel-head status** string as *"Grounded · cites its sources"* (line 218: `head: '... + title + "Grounded · cites its sources" status ...'`; restated line 406). `EXPERIENCE.md` specifies a Guide **per-answer footnote** as *"Grounded in the record · zero ungrounded claims"* (line 142: *A quiet "Grounded in the record · zero ungrounded claims" footnote*). These are two different strings, both serving a "grounded" assurance role, and neither spine cross-references the other. They are plausibly two distinct surfaces (a persistent head status vs. a per-message footnote), in which case both are fine — but as written, an implementer cannot tell whether this is intentional (two strings, two locations) or drift (one assurance line that mutated across spines).
*Fix:* In whichever spine owns the canonical string, state explicitly that these are **two distinct elements** — the persistent panel-head status (`"Grounded · cites its sources"`, DESIGN) and the per-answer footnote (`"Grounded in the record · zero ungrounded claims"`, EXPERIENCE) — and cross-reference each from the other; or, if only one was intended, reconcile to a single string. (Note: "zero ungrounded claims" is a confident, defensible, cited-by-construction claim — it ties directly to SM-C3 — so no tone problem either way; this is purely a one-vs-two ambiguity.)

### Low

**2. Guide thinking-state line differs across spines (template vs. instance) without a note tying them.**
`DESIGN.md` line 221 gives the thinking-state copy as a calm *"Reading the record"* line; `EXPERIENCE.md` line 141 gives *"Reading: loandemo case study · Glass Box artifacts"*. These are compatible — EXPERIENCE marks its version with "e.g." (an example instance of a dynamic "what it is reading" line), and DESIGN's "Reading the record" reads as the generic/empty-state label — so this is **not a contradiction**. But the two spines never state the relationship, so a literal reader could mistake them for conflicting fixed strings.
*Fix:* Add a half-sentence in DESIGN (or EXPERIENCE) clarifying that `"Reading the record"` is the generic label and `"Reading: <named sources>"` is the populated, per-message form — same element, two render states.

**3. Bio-end positioning string is lowercase (`"seasoned, building at the frontier."`) while the canonical positioning is Title-case.**
`EXPERIENCE.md` line 107 states both copy-paste bios end *"seasoned, building at the frontier."* (lowercase, trailing period). This is **grammatically correct** — it is the tail of a running sentence inside prose bios, not the hero `<h1>` — and the canonical positioning *"Seasoned, building at the frontier"* (the `<h1>`, line 104) is correctly Title-case. So this is intended, not an error. Flagging only because a literal implementer copying strings between the hero and the bios could "normalize" one to match the other and break the intended casing.
*Fix:* Add an inline note at line 107 (e.g. "lowercased mid-sentence by design; the standalone hero/h1 form is Title-case") so the casing difference is documented as deliberate.
