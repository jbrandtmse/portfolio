The Master Timeline — the organizing spine, as a craft-arc.

The site's organizing spine and a key showpiece ("one click in, not the front door"), rendered 1:1 on the locked identity (all Source Serif 4, warm cream + Prussian navy, hairline rules, faint drafting grid, flat). Accessibility / fallback notes — feeds EXPERIENCE.md

  • The arc is a presentation of a real <ol> of dated entries (eras, ticks, flagships, Dots) — it works with JS off and degrades to a plain ordered list; the horizontal layout is CSS-only (no JavaScript).
  • The spine, era bands, and connecting lines are decorative (drawn with pseudo-elements / aria-hidden): no information lives only in the graphics — dates, era labels, and link text carry the meaning in the DOM.
  • Every Dot is a real link — loandemo's Dots drill into /work/loandemo#… readers; the portfolio's Dots cross-link into the Glass Box (/glass-box/…). Keyboard-focusable, screen-reader navigable, no JS required to follow.
  • Era bands are labeled regions (each <li> carries an aria-label) so the runway vs the agentic turn read as distinct sections to assistive tech.
  • Orientation is presentation only: the SAME ordered list reflows from a horizontal arc (desktop) to a vertical list (mobile). Reading order (oldest → newest) is preserved in the DOM either way.
  • The live flagship's halo ring is static (no animation) — reduced-motion safe; "Live" is also stated as text in the pill.
  • The S2 semantic-zoom control is a non-interactive preview in S1 (labeled as such); the S1 arc presents both zoom levels at once, so no information is gated behind a gesture.
  • Color is never the sole signal: runway vs turn vs flagship is also conveyed by era labels, dates, faint-vs-filled treatment, and link text. Navy on cream meets AA (10.15:1); secondary ink meets AA (5.6:1). The cross-link to /glass-box and the static fallback footer keep the page reachable without the arc.
