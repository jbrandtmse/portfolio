# PRD Quality Review — Josh Brandt Portfolio Site

## Overall verdict

This is a strong chain-top PRD that earns its length: it has a real thesis ("Seasoned, Not Stuck"; proof-as-process; "the medium is the message"), names the trade-off it bets on (no technology moat — "the real risk is execution and taste, not competitors"), and carries that thesis cleanly down into staged FRs, NFRs, named UJs, a verbatim Glossary, and counter-metrics. Mechanically it is near-spotless: FR-1…36 / NFR-1…7 / SM / UJ IDs are all contiguous and cross-refs resolve, and the `[S1/S2/S3]` stage tags match the §12 MVP scope lists exactly. The two real risks for downstream are (1) a handful of S1 FRs whose "done" depends on content/decisions still parked in Open Questions (talk inventory, channel URLs, email transport, response-time SLA), and (2) one S3 FR (FR-18) plus one NFR (NFR-4) that are under-specified for "done" — minor given S3 is intentionally exploratory, but worth tightening before stories.

## Decision-readiness — strong

A decision-maker can act on this. The pivotal calls are stated *as* decisions and carry their rejected alternative: code-as-CMS over a CMS (`#34`), curated Demonstrations over live arbitrary execution (`#8`→`#35`), git single-source over live external reads (`#37`), highlight-reel narrative kept distinct from honest process exposure (`#11`). The addendum's "Rejected / calibrated alternatives" section reinforces these rather than re-litigating them. The Vision is unusually honest about what is *given up*: "There is no defensible *technology* moat" (§1) is a trade-off most PRDs would bury. Priority is asserted, not hedged — §3.1 commits "practitioner amplification is the *upstream engine*" and explains the causal chain (peer shares → organizer conversions), instead of declaring all three personas equally important.

The Open Questions (§14) are genuinely open — final stack (Astro vs Next), Stage 2/3 cut-line, per-feature "how agentic at launch" — not rhetorical questions answered in the next sentence. Counter-metrics (SM-C1…C4) are the clearest decision-readiness signal: they encode what *not* to optimize (don't bury the organizer, don't slip the ship, zero ungrounded claims, shares over volume), each tied to the SM it counterbalances.

One soft edge: §6's "agentic ideal, graceful fallback" defers the per-feature agentic-vs-fallback call to Open Question 9 "for features where it's unsettled" without saying *which* features are unsettled — a `[NOTE FOR PM]` enumerating them would sharpen the handoff. Not a blocker.

## Substance over theater — strong

Little furniture here. The four JTBD entries (§3.1) each drive structure: the practitioner sets the default experience, the organizer forces the fast-credible path and SM-C1, the wanderer justifies the Creative Wing, and Josh-the-builder is the reason code-as-CMS exists. None is decorative, and the count stays at four. The Glossary is working vocabulary (21 defined terms, all reused — `Advocate Agent` ×25, `Master Timeline` ×21, `Invite-Me` ×20), not a thoroughness prop.

The differentiation claim is earned, not template-driven: §1 grounds novelty in "the rare combination of a 30-year track record **and** cutting-edge practice" and §2 ties timing to "the authentic public build history (`#39`) is the one thing a competitor cannot retrofit." NFRs carry product-specific thresholds, not boilerplate — NFR-1 names FCP < ~2s, JS < ~200–250KB gzipped, one fixed WebGL canvas, KTX2/Basis/Draco; NFR-2 specifies the actual a11y contract (`role="dialog"`, transcript `role="log"` + `aria-live="polite"` batched per-message). Banned-adjective sweep is clean: no "gracefully / reasonable performance / user-friendly / seamless / robust / intuitive" anywhere in the FRs.

### Findings
- **low** NFR-7 leans on adjective-free but unmeasured "sufficient" ("server logs *sufficient to* measure §13 metrics") (§8) — *Note:* acceptable as a constraint NFR, but the one place an observability bound could be stated (retrieval-miss rate that triggers embeddings) is left implicit. *Fix:* name the miss-rate threshold that flips the embeddings decision, since FR-6/NFR-4 already gesture at it.

## Strategic coherence — strong

The PRD has a single thesis and the features serve it. "Proof-as-Process" is the spine: the Glass Box renders *real* `_bmad-output/` artifacts (FR-13), the Master Timeline is *git-harvested* with "no manual re-entry" (FR-16), and UJ-1's climax — "the site they're touring *is the project on the timeline they're scrubbing*" — is the thesis made literal. Prioritization follows the thesis, not ease: §5 declares "the **Advocate Agent and Proof-as-Process are the soul** … When effort is scarce, protect the soul first," and the soul tier (agent + Glass Box) is loaded into Stage 1, not deferred to the "magic" stage.

Success Metrics validate the thesis rather than measuring activity. SM-1 is a speaking outcome, SM-2 is "Josh pitches ≥1 new conference — ideally *a talk about the site itself*" (the recursion as a metric), SM-3 is net-new follows. Crucially the PRD avoids the DAU/MAU tell the rubric warns about — SM-C4 explicitly rejects raw volume ("A spike in low-intent cold traffic is not success"). Each SM cross-references the FRs it validates. The MVP scope-kind is coherent: an *experience/credibility* cut (Stage 1 = "Credible Hub"), with §12.3 stating the scope logic — "Stage = delivery sequence, not priority cut" — so the full-depth S2/S3 specification reads as sequencing, not gold-plating.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate

This is the dimension carrying the most residual risk, though it's well above average. All 36 FRs have an explicit **Consequences (testable)** block, and most are genuinely verifiable — FR-6 ("retrieval score below threshold → canned response and **does not call the model**"), FR-7 ("verifiable via `view-source` + find, JS disabled"), FR-31 ("creates a Postgres row **and** triggers an email"), FR-35 ("passes the Rich Results Test"). These will source-extract into acceptance criteria cleanly.

The gaps are concentrated and identifiable:

### Findings
- **medium** FR-18 (Site-wide semantic zoom, S3) has *no* testable consequence — its sole bullet is `[ASSUMPTION: zoom level changes the granularity … exact behavior defined in UX]` (§7.4) — *Note:* "done" is undefined; the FR is a placeholder. Tolerable because it is S3 (out of MVP) and explicitly punts to UX, but it should not reach story creation in this state. *Fix:* either give it one concrete acceptance condition or mark it explicitly as a UX-blocked stub pending §14 Open Question.
- **medium** NFR-4 is titled "Agent reliability **& latency**" but states no latency bound (§8) — "Responses stream" is a behavior, not a budget; there is no TTFB / p95 target for the one live, user-facing dynamic surface. *Note:* for an agent-first product this is the NFR an engineer will most want a number on. *Fix:* add a streaming-start (time-to-first-token) target and a hard ceiling, even if approximate ("~first token < 1s on the VM endpoint").
- **medium** Several S1 FRs are testable in *form* but not yet *checkable* because their content is unresolved — FR-19 ("`[ASSUMPTION: 3–5 signature talks … exact list is content-inventory]`"), FR-20 ("`[ASSUMPTION: real testimonials/logos … placeholders flagged]`"), FR-23 ("`[ASSUMPTION: specific channel URLs/handles pending]`"), FR-31 (response-time "N TBD", email transport "TBD") (§7.5/7.6/7.8). *Note:* these are correctly tagged and indexed, so it is honest, not hidden — but they are S1/MVP, so a story team hits them on day one. *Fix:* land the §14 Open-Question 3/5 content inventory + transport/SLA decisions before or at the start of the epics pass; they gate MVP "done," not just polish.
- **low** FR-21 EPK "serves a current PDF reflecting the signature talks and bios" (§7.5) — "current" has no freshness mechanism in a no-CMS build. *Fix:* state that the EPK is build-generated from the same source as FR-19/FR-20 so "current" is structural, not manual.

## Scope honesty — strong

Omissions are explicit and do real work. §11 Non-Goals draws hard lines (no CMS, no live runtime reads, no public live execution, not a lead-gen funnel, not a static CV) and the two that could be *silently* assumed carry the right callouts: the job-seeking surface is `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` ("architected for but not built") and the podcast carries a `[NOTE FOR PM: emotionally load-bearing — revisit if the podcast ships]`. The §3.2 Non-Users section ("Hiring managers / recruiters / clients — explicitly *not* a current target") prevents a whole class of mis-scoped FRs. De-scoping is done out loud: §12.2 lists every deferred FR with a one-line "what it is," and §12.3 frames staging as sequence not cut, so nothing is dropped silently.

Open-items density is appropriate for the stakes. Counts: 10 Open Questions, ~23 distinct inline `[ASSUMPTION]` tags, 1 `[NOTE FOR PM]`, 1 `[NON-GOAL for MVP]`. For a launch-stakes, three-stage, full-FR-depth PRD this is *proportionate to surface area*, not alarming — and critically, the unresolved items cluster in S2/S3 and in content/config (talk list, handles, transport), not in the load-bearing S1 architecture or the thesis. The PRD also reconciles its own tensions rather than smoothing them: §7.2 explicitly reconciles "the agent's *visible thinking* is a designed wow" against NFR-2's anti-spam a11y rule ("announce per-message, not per-token"), and §6 guards the early Speaker-Surface placement with SM-C1 against cinematic re-order regressions.

### Findings
- **low** Open Question 9 ("Per-feature 'agentic ideal, graceful fallback' calls") is a recurring decision spread across many FRs but the PRD does not enumerate which FRs are unsettled (§14) — *Fix:* list the affected FR-Ns so the architect/UX pass knows the exact decision set rather than re-deriving it.

## Downstream usability — strong

As a chain-top PRD feeding bmad-ux → architecture → epics, this is built to be source-extracted. §0 names each downstream consumer and what it pulls. The Glossary is declared binding ("Introducing a synonym anywhere is a discipline violation") and the discipline holds — every domain noun is used identically across FRs/UJs/SMs (verified: no case/plural drift; "Demonstrator" the feature vs "Demonstrations" its instances is a deliberate, defined distinction, not drift). FR/NFR/SM/UJ IDs are contiguous, unique, and every cross-reference resolves (no dangling FR-37+, no orphan SM/UJ refs). Sections are largely self-contained — FRs cite NFRs and UJs by ID and the Glossary by term, not "see above." All four UJs have named protagonists (Devon/Mara/Sam/Josh) carrying persona, entry state, path, climax, resolution, and an edge case inline; no floating UJs. §5 (Aesthetic & Tone) and §6 (IA) are pre-positioned explicitly "for `bmad-ux`," and §10 records the stack as input "pending architecture," which is exactly the right handoff posture.

The one frictful spot for a downstream reader is the assumptions roundtrip (two orphans — see Mechanical notes), which is `low` because both orphans are UX-deferred (palette, semantic-zoom behavior) and would surface in the UX pass anyway.

## Shape fit — strong

The shape matches the product. This is a consumer/branded, UX-heavy, agentic experience, so named UJs with protagonists are load-bearing — and the PRD treats them that way (FRs declare "Realizes UJ-N"; UJ-1's recursion *is* the product's reason to exist). It is *not* over-formalized: there is no UJ-density-for-a-single-operator problem; even Josh-the-builder (the one operator-ish role, UJ-4) earns its UJ because the maintenance model is part of the consumer-facing story ("the site grew by being engineered, not edited"). It is *not* under-formalized: a consumer product with a cinematic layer correctly gets §5 Aesthetic/Tone and §6 IA as first-class load-bearing sections rather than an afterthought. The deliberate full-FR depth across all three stages is the right shape for the stated intent (downstream must not make S1 decisions that block S2/S3 magic), and §12.3 defends that choice rather than leaving it as unexplained scope bloat. Solo-built rigor is calibrated: heavy where it compounds (Glossary, IDs, NFR thresholds, counter-metrics), appropriately light where it would be ceremony.
