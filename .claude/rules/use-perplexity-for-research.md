# Use Perplexity for Research

Whenever you are unsure of a fact, or unsure how to do something, use the Perplexity MCP tools to research before answering or acting. Do not guess or rely on potentially stale knowledge.

## When to use

- You are uncertain whether a fact is true or current.
- You don't know how to do something (an API, library, tool, config, or technique).
- Information may have changed since your knowledge cutoff (versions, releases, pricing, news).
- You are about to state something with low confidence.

## Which tool

- `perplexity_search` — find URLs, facts, and recent news quickly.
- `perplexity_ask` — quick AI-answered questions with citations.
- `perplexity_research` — in-depth, multi-source investigation (slow, 30s+); use for thorough research.
- `perplexity_reason` — complex analysis requiring step-by-step logic.

## How to apply

1. Recognize the uncertainty before responding.
2. Query Perplexity with a specific question.
3. Cite or incorporate the verified information in your answer.
4. Prefer verified, sourced answers over guesses — when in doubt, look it up.
