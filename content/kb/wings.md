## How the Wings compose with the Guide

The Wings are the default browsable structure — fully self-navigable with JavaScript off. A visitor can reach any Wing route directly from the footer, /browse, or the sitemap.

The Guide can blend across Wings on demand: ask "show me the creative work" or "what technical projects do you have?" and the Guide grounds its answer in this KB content and links the relevant Wing route. The Guide does not gate the Wings — it augments them.
